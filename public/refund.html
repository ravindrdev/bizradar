Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Our commitment

At GrowthIM we want you to be completely satisfied with your market intelligence report.

If something goes wrong on our end we will make it right quickly and without hassle.

When you get a full refund

You are entitled to a full refund in the following situations:

Report failed to generate

If our system fails to generate your report due to a technical error on our side and no report appears in your dashboard.

Charged but no report received

If your payment was processed successfully but you never received your report in your dashboard within 30 minutes.

Duplicate charge

If you were charged twice for the same report due to a payment processing error.

Completely wrong report

If our system generated a report for a completely different business despite you entering the correct business name and address.

When you do not get a refund

Refunds are NOT provided in the following situations:

Report successfully delivered

If your report was generated and is visible in your dashboard we cannot offer a refund.

Wrong information entered

If you entered an incorrect business name or wrong address and the report was generated for the wrong business as a result.

Change of mind

If you simply changed your mind after the report was generated.

Dissatisfied with content

If you do not agree with the recommendations or analysis in the report.

Different expectations

If the report did not match what you expected but was successfully generated.

After 7 days

Refund requests submitted more than 7 days after purchase date will not be considered.

How to request a refund

Follow these steps to request a refund:

Step 1 — Email us

Send an email to: support@growthim.com

Subject line: Refund Request - [Your Order ID]

Include in your email:

  • Your account email address
  • Your Order ID (found in your payment receipt)
  • Reason for refund request
  • Screenshot if applicable

Step 2 — We review your request

We will review your request within 2 business days and reply to your email with our decision.

Step 3 — Refund is processed

If your refund is approved it will be processed within 5 to 7 business days back to your original payment method.

Processing time depends on your bank or card provider.

Partial refunds

We do not offer partial refunds. Refunds are either full ($12.99) or not approved.

Report access after refund

If your refund is approved your account access to the report will be removed.

Report expiry

Reports are saved in your dashboard for 1 year from the date of generation.

We do not offer refunds for reports that have expired after the 1 year period.

Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our refund decision you may email us again at: support@growthim.com

Write ESCALATION REQUEST in the subject line and your case will be reviewed by a senior team member within 3 business days.

Contact

For all refund related queries: support@growthim.com

We aim to respond within 24 hours.
