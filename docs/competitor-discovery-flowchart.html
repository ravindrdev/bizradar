    FNC --> CACHE{"Cache check

    REV_CHECK{"NAICS 722xxx?

    REV_CHECK -->|"Yes"| FETCH_REV["Fetch subject reviews

    REV_CHECK -->|"No"| BUILD_Q
    FETCH_REV --> BUILD_Q

    BUILD_Q["buildCompetitorQuery()

    BUILD_Q --> Q_WITH["queryWithLocality

    Q_WITH --> LADDER_START
    Q_WITHOUT --> LADDER_START

    LADDER_START --> S1

Phase 4 — Post-Processing, Enrichment & Output

flowchart TD
    POOL(["Raw pool from radius ladder
e.g. 12 competitors"]) --> DEDUP

    DEDUP["fuzzyDedupByName()

    SORT["competitorComparator() sort

    SORT --> SLICE
