Phase 4 — Post-Processing, Enrichment & Output
1. fuzzyDedupByName()Normalized name dedup (lowercase, alpha+space, 20-char cap, first-seen wins)
↓
2. competitorComparator() sortPRIMARY: distance bucket (<2mi → <5mi → <15mi → 15mi+ → unknown)
SECONDARY: higher rating first — TERTIARY: more reviews first
Closest competitors ALWAYS rank top regardless of rating
↓
3. Slice into tierstop7 = [0:7] • top5 = [0:5] • top3 = [0:3]
↓
4. Enrich Top 5 — parallel getDetails()Per competitor (24hr cache): 3 reviews (300 char), address, weekday_text (hours)
↓
5. classifyCompetitorTier() — per competitor vs subject
Subject < 25 reviews (SMALL)
TTHREAT — comp rating ≥ 4.5 AND comp reviews ≥ 10
NNEUTRAL — comp reviews < 5 (too few to judge)
WWINNING — comp rating < 3.8 AND comp reviews ≥ 5
NNEUTRAL — everything else
Subject ≥ 25 reviews (ESTABLISHED)
ratingGap = comp.rating - subject.rating
TTHREAT — gap ≥ 0.5 AND comp reviews ≥ 20
WWINNING — gap ≤ -0.3 OR comp rating < 3.8
NNEUTRAL — everything else (filtered from report)
↓
6. Multi-sort review fetch for Top 5 (parallel)Replaces single-sort with: most relevant, newest, highest, lowest
Gives Claude richer evidence for competitor analysis in report
