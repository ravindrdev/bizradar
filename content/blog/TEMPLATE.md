Open with the direct answer in the first 100 to 150 words. State the main point plainly in the first paragraph so a reader, and an AI search engine, gets the answer immediately. Then expand below.

## A section heading

A normal paragraph. Use **bold** to emphasize the words that matter. Link to your own pages so visitors and search engines move deeper into the site, like the [report tool](/app) or the [business types page](/reports).

### A smaller sub heading

More detail goes here. You can use bullet lists:

- A first point
- A second point
- A third point

And numbered lists when order matters:

1. First do this
2. Then do that

> Use a quote block like this to highlight a key takeaway.

## A closing section

Wrap up with a clear next step. A good blog post ends by pointing the reader somewhere useful, such as [running a market analysis](/app).
